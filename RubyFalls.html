Ruby Falls

Location

Ruby Falls is a breathtaking underground waterfall located within Lookout Mountain, near Chattanooga, Tennessee, USA. It is situated within the Lookout Mountain Caverns, beneath the surface of Lookout Mountain.

History

Leo Lambert's accidental discovery of Ruby Falls in 1928 marked the beginning of its storied history as a renowned tourist attraction. Lambert, originally from Indiana, was drawn to the natural beauty and geological formations of Lookout Mountain in Tennessee. He was an avid cave explorer and saw potential in the cave system for public enjoyment.

During one of his expeditions into Lookout Mountain Caverns, Lambert noticed a peculiar sound of rushing water echoing through the cavern. Curious, he followed the sound deeper into the cave system until he stumbled upon the breathtaking sight of Ruby Falls, a stunning underground waterfall cascading from the ceiling of the cavern.

Impressed by the beauty and grandeur of the waterfall, Lambert was inspired to share this marvel with the public. He named the waterfall after his wife, Ruby, to commemorate her and her support for his explorations. Recognizing the potential for tourism, Lambert embarked on a project to develop the cave system for public access.

Lambert's vision included creating safe pathways, installing lighting systems to showcase the cave's features, and providing guided tours to educate visitors about the geological wonders hidden beneath Lookout Mountain. In 1930, Ruby Falls officially opened to the public, attracting tourists from across the country and beyond.

Today, Ruby Falls remains a testament to Leo Lambert's vision and passion for exploration. It continues to draw visitors who seek adventure, education, and a deeper appreciation for the wonders of the natural world. As one of the most popular attractions in the Southeastern United States, Ruby Falls holds a special place in the hearts of both locals and tourists alike, leaving a lasting legacy that celebrates the marvels of nature.

Climate

The stable climate within the Ruby Falls Caverns is a result of its subterranean location, which provides insulation from external weather conditions.

Temperature Stability: The temperature inside Ruby Falls Caverns remains relatively constant throughout the year, hovering around 60°F (15°C). This stability is due to the insulating properties of the surrounding rock formations, which help regulate the temperature within the cave.

Protection from External Elements

Being underground shields the caverns from the fluctuations in temperature and weather conditions experienced on the surface. This protection ensures that visitors can enjoy a comfortable environment regardless of the season, making Ruby Falls a popular attraction year-round.

Notable Features of Ruby Falls Caverns

The Cathedral Room is a large chamber within Ruby Falls Caverns, renowned for its acoustics. The chamber's vast size and unique geological features contribute to its exceptional sound quality, making it a popular venue for special events and musical performances.

The Christmas Tree is a striking formation within the caverns, resembling the shape of a decorated Christmas tree. This natural wonder is created by mineral deposits over thousands of years, and its intricate structure captivates visitors with its beauty and symbolism.

The Frozen Niagara is another notable formation within Ruby Falls Caverns, named for its resemblance to a frozen waterfall. This stunning display of stalactites and flowstone formations resembles the cascading layers of a frozen waterfall, creating a breathtaking sight for visitors to behold.

Climate

The area has a relatively stable average temperature with a peak at 21 °C (70 °F) in November to January, and a low of 13 °C (55 °F) in June. The nights are cold all through the year, with temperatures between −9 and 5 °C (16 and 41 °F). The relative humidity is rather low and constant throughout the year at 30% to 45%. The rainfall is also low at 1 to 3 mm (0.039 to 0.118 in) per month between April and November, but it may increase up to 80 mm (3.1 in) in January. However, except for January, even in the rainy season the number of rainy days is fewer than 5 per month.

Exploration and Access:

Visitors to Ruby Falls can explore the caverns through guided tours provided by Ruby Falls, LLC. These tours lead visitors through narrow passageways and chambers adorned with spectacular geological formations, culminating in the awe-inspiring sight of Ruby Falls itself. The journey to the waterfall typically involves a moderate amount of walking and may include some stairs and low-ceilinged areas.

Tour Experience:

During the guided tour, visitors learn about the geological processes that formed the cave system and the fascinating history of its discovery. Guides also provide insights into the diverse ecosystem that exists within the cave, including unique species of plants and animals adapted to the subterranean environment.
